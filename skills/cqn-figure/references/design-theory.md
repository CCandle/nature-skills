# Design Theory

Colour, typography, and layout guidelines for engineering figures.

## Colour

- Use colour to encode meaningful differences, not decoration
- In engineering-report mode: one colour per signal type, linestyle to
  differentiate conditions
- In high-impact-paper mode: restrained palette with one neutral, one
  signal, one accent family
- Avoid red-green encoding (colourblind accessibility)
- Test in grayscale before finalising

## Typography

- Sans-serif for figure text (Arial / Helvetica / DejaVu Sans)
- Axis labels: 10-11 pt
- Tick labels: 8-9 pt
- Annotations: 8 pt
- Panel labels: bold, 10 pt

## Layout

- Single-column figures: 3.5 inch (89 mm) wide
- Double-column figures: 7 inch (183 mm) wide
- Height: as needed, but prefer 2:3 to 3:2 aspect ratio
- Multi-panel: align panels by shared axes when possible
- Minimum whitespace between panels: 0.1 inch
