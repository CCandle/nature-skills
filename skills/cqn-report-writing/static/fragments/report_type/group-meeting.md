# Group Meeting Report

Weekly or biweekly progress update for your supervisor and group.

## Purpose

Communicate what was tried, what worked, what failed, and what needs
input. The audience has limited time and context.

## Structure

| Section | Purpose | Length |
|---|---|---|
| Previous goals | What was planned since last meeting | 1-2 bullet points |
| What was done | Experiments, analysis, or development completed | 3-5 bullet points |
| Key results | The most important findings, with evidence | 3-5 sentences + figure if relevant |
| Failures / blockers | What didn't work and why | 2-3 bullet points |
| Next steps | What will be done before next meeting | 2-3 bullet points |
| Help needed | Decisions or input needed from supervisor | 1-2 questions |

## Emphasis

- Results first, methods brief
- A single good figure is worth more than a paragraph of text
- Blocker that needs supervisor input should be clearly flagged
- Keep it to 1 page or 3-4 slides max

## Tone

- Direct: "Tested X. Found Y. Not yet Z."
- No promotional language: "We are excited to report..." → "Measured X = Y under condition Z."
