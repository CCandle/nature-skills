# Waveform Evidence Guidelines

How to handle oscilloscope captures, screenshots, and data files in lab records.

## Minimum requirements

A waveform claim should reference:
- **File name** of the CSV, screenshot, or capture
- **Channel identification** (ch1, ch2, diff probe A)
- **Measurement context** (trigger point, timebase, vertical scale)
- **What was observed** (peak voltage, frequency, anomaly description)

## Example

> Observation: Phase A voltage shows 5-ring at turn-off [CF]
> Evidence: scope_capture_001.csv ch1 @ 50V/div 10us/div, probe 10x
