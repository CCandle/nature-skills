# Speaker Notes Guidelines

## What to include

For each slide, write 2-4 sentences of speaker notes:

1. **Opening line** — what to say when the slide appears
2. **Key point** — the single most important message
3. **Pointer** — what to indicate on the slide (e.g. "the red trace is before")
4. **Anticipated question** — prepare for the most likely question

## Example

Slide title: "ADC ENOB improves with external Vref"
Speaker notes:
"With the board LDO, we measured 9.2 effective bits. After switching to
the ADR4520 reference, ENOB improved to 10.8 bits. [Point to the two
traces.] That's 1.6 bits of improvement from Vref noise alone. The
remaining noise is now limited by the ADC quantisation.
Question I expect: 'What about temperature drift?' We haven't tested
that yet — it's our next experiment."
