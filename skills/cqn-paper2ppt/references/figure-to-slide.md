# Figure-to-Slide Guidelines

## Placing figures on slides

- One figure per results slide; maximum two if they directly compare
- Figure should occupy 50-70% of slide area
- Text should be minimal — the figure is the evidence

## Figure annotations for slides

- Increase font size compared to paper: 12-14 pt for axis labels
- Add coloured arrows or boxes to highlight key features
- Add a brief title at the top of the figure: "Before vs After Vref Change"

## Caption on slides

- Short: "ENOB = 9.2 bit (LDO Vref) vs 10.8 bit (ADR4520)"
- Full details go in speaker notes
