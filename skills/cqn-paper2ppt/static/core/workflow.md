# Presentation Workflow

## Step 1: Gather material

Collect the source material: paper, lab records, analysis reports,
figures, or project notes. Identify what is available and what is missing.

## Step 2: Identify deck type

Apply the deck_type fragment to determine the slide structure and
emphasis.

## Step 3: Build slide outline per deck type

Follow the slide sequence defined in the deck_type fragment.
Each slide slot gets a working title and a list of key points.

## Step 4: Add evidence references

For each results slide, link to the data source, lab record, or paper
figure. Declare measurement parameters in the slide or speaker notes.

## Step 5: Write speaker notes

Every slide must have speaker notes. Notes should include:
- What to say (2-3 sentences)
- What to point at on the slide
- What question to expect from the audience

## Step 6: Review for missing reproducibility

For paper presentations, explicitly check what experimental details
are missing from the paper and list them on a dedicated slide.

## Step 7: Optional .pptx export

If the user requests a file, provide python-pptx code.
Do not install python-pptx — it is not pre-installed.
