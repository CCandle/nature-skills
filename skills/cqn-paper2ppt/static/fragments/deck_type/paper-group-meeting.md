# Paper Group Meeting Deck

Journal club or paper reading presentation for a research paper.

## Slide sequence

| # | Slide title | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title | Paper title, authors, venue, date, presenter |
| 2 | Research Problem | What problem does the paper address? |
| 3 | Core Idea | One-sentence summary of the proposed approach |
| 4 | Method / Architecture | System diagram or method pipeline |
| 5 | Experimental Setup | Hardware, dataset, parameters, baseline |
| 6 | Key Results | Main results table, figure, or metric comparison |
| 7 | Strengths | What the paper does well |
| 8 | Weaknesses / Missing Info | Missing Reproducibility Information slide |
| 9 | Relevance to My Project | What can I use or learn? |
| 10 | Discussion Questions | 2-3 open questions for group discussion |

## Notes

- Slide 8 must include Missing Reproducibility Information: hardware
  version, firmware, sampling rate, dataset, baseline details not provided
  in the paper.
- Speaker notes should explain why each missing detail matters.
