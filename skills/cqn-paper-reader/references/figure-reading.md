# Figure Reading

## What to extract from figures

- Type: waveform, FFT, bar chart, schematic, photo
- Axes labels and units
- Key features: peaks, settling time, THD, noise floor
- What the figure claims to show vs what it actually shows

## When the paper does not provide source data

- Describe the figure qualitatively
- Note that quantitative values are estimated from the figure
  and not verified
- Do not extract precise values from low-resolution figure images
