# Missing Information Guidelines

## How to handle missing information

When the paper does not provide a piece of information:

| Situation | What to write |
|---|---|
| Hardware not specified | `Hardware platform: Not specified` |
| Sampling rate not given | `fs: Missing` |
| Dataset not named | `Dataset: Not specified` |
| No ablation study | `Ablation: None reported` |
| No error bars | `Error bars: Not provided` |

## What not to do

- Do not infer sampling rate from figure time axes
- Do not assume standard hardware
- Do not fill dataset details from your knowledge of the field
- Do not write "probably" or "likely" for missing parameters
