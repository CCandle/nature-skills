# Stance

## Read for reproducibility, not just comprehension

An engineering paper is useful if it can be reproduced. Focus on
extracting what is needed to reproduce the results.

## Distinguish claims from evidence

A paper may claim "the method achieves 95% accuracy" but the evidence
may only support "the method achieves 95% on a specific dataset under
specific conditions." Note the difference.

## Do not fill gaps

If the sampling rate is not given, write `Missing`. Do not calculate
it from the time axis of a figure. Do not assume standard values.
