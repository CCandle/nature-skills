# cqn-paper-reader

Read and analyse engineering research papers. Extracts method, experimental
setup, reproducibility information, and research usefulness.

## Usage

Provide a paper (PDF, DOI, arXiv, URL, or text):

> Read this paper: https://doi.org/10.1109/TPEL.2026.012345

Output: structured Paper Reading Note with Missing Reproducibility
Information and research usefulness assessment.

## Key rules

- No fabricated information — if the paper doesn't say it, write Missing
- Distinguishes claims from evidence
- Includes "What is useful for my research" section
