# Restriction Language

Standard phrasings for different access scenarios.

## Fully open

"All data, code, and firmware are publicly available at [repository/DOI]
under a [license] license."

## Available on request

"The raw measurement data and analysis scripts are available from the
corresponding author upon reasonable request."

## Partially restricted

"The ADC measurement data and analysis scripts are available at [DOI].
The FPGA bitstream contains proprietary IP and cannot be shared.
The firmware source for the non-proprietary modules is available on
request."

## No public data

"No new data were generated. The analysis is based on simulation models
described in the Method section."

Or:

"The data that support the findings of this study are available from
the corresponding author upon reasonable request. The firmware and
bitstream are proprietary and cannot be shared."
