# High-Impact Writing Workflow

## Step 1: Identify target journal
Confirm the specific journal with the user.

## Step 2: Review source material
Check that there is sufficient evidence for the claims. If the draft
has too many `[RH]` or `[UG]` claims, flag this before proceeding.

## Step 3: Restructure introduction
- Open with broad motivation accessible to nonspecialists
- Narrow to the specific problem
- State the contribution clearly

## Step 4: Restructure results
- Highlight the main finding first
- Use hero figure or summary table
- Supporting evidence in secondary panels

## Step 5: Verify all claims
- Every [CF] must have traceable evidence
- Every [RH] must be labelled
- Missing experiments must be marked
