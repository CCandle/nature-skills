# Safety Checklist

Before finalising any experiment plan involving electrical hazards,
check all of the following:

## High-voltage safety (>48 V)

- [ ] Isolation transformer on DUT power
- [ ] Differential probes only (no ground-referenced probes on HV)
- [ ] Clear discharge path for DC bus capacitors
- [ ] Emergency stop within arm's reach
- [ ] No lone working

## Current safety (>1 A)

- [ ] Wire gauge adequate for expected current
- [ ] Fuse or circuit breaker on supply
- [ ] Current probe rated for expected peak + margin
- [ ] Connections tightened and insulated

## General

- [ ] Work area clear of conductive debris
- [ ] Fire extinguisher accessible
- [ ] First aid kit accessible
- [ ] Lab partner or supervisor informed
