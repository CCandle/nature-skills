# Chinese Thesis Style

## Sections
Numbered in Chinese decimal: 1, 1.1, 1.1.1.
Chinese abstract first, then English abstract.

## Citations
Various accepted: numbered [1], or author-year.
Reference list at end.

## Figures
Label: "图 1" (Chinese) or "Figure 1" (English). Caption centred below.
Tables: "表 1" (Chinese) or "Table 1". Caption centred above.

## Format
Single column, A4 paper. Print binding margin.
