# Nature Style

## Sections
Unnumbered. Introduction, Results, Discussion, Methods (after main text).
Online-only Methods section.

## Citations
Numbered: 1, 2, 3. Reference list at end, numbered.
Author names: up to 5 authors listed, et al. beyond.

## Figures
Label: "Figure 1." Caption below.
Tables: "Table 1." Caption above.

## Abstract
Single paragraph, 150 words max. No citations, no abbreviations.
Broad audience: accessible to a nonspecialist.

## Format
Single column, ~21 cm × 28 cm page. Figures at 89 mm (single column) or
183 mm (double column).
