# Stance

## Claims need evidence

A paper that makes claims without supporting evidence will be rejected
by reviewers. Every claim in the draft must be traceable to a data
file, lab record, or cited reference. If not yet verified, mark it.

## Engineering style

- Use active voice for describing your work: "We measured..."
- Use passive voice for standard procedures: "The ADC was configured..."
- Define every abbreviation at first use
- Keep paragraphs short (3-5 sentences)
- One idea per paragraph

## No promotional language

Do not use: "novel", "first-ever", "groundbreaking", "state-of-the-art"
(to describe your own work). Let the evidence speak.
