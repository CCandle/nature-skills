# Paper Writing Workflow

## Step 1: Gather inputs

Collect experimental data, lab records, analysis reports, figures,
and any prior drafts. Note what evidence is available and what is
missing.

## Step 2: Identify sections

Determine which section(s) to draft. Load the corresponding
fragment(s).

## Step 3: Build evidence table

Map each claim to its evidence source. Tag claims:

| Claim | Evidence | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| ADC achieves 10.8 ENOB | lab-record-20260609 | [CF] |
| External Vref reduces noise | ADR4520 datasheet | [RH] |

## Step 4: Draft per section

Apply the section-specific fragment for structure and conventions.
Write around the evidence table — each claim in the narrative should
correspond to an evidence row.

## Step 5: Mark missing evidence

If a planned claim has no evidence, mark it in the draft:
`[Missing evidence — measurement planned for next phase]`

## Step 6: Review

Check:
- Every claim has an evidence tag
- Missing evidence is explicitly marked
- No unsupported novelty claims
- All abbreviations defined
- Units and notation consistent
