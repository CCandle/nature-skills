# Conclusion

## Structure
1. **Summary of contribution** — what was done, with key result [CF]
2. **Limitations** — what the work does not cover
3. **Future work** — what could be done next

## Rules
- Do not repeat the abstract verbatim
- Limitations are mandatory — a conclusion without limitations
  looks like overclaiming
- Future work should be concrete, not generic ("further research
  is needed" is not helpful)
- End with the broader significance, not a cliché
