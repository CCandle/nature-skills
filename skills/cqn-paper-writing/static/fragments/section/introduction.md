# Introduction

## Structure
1. **Broad context** — the engineering domain and why it matters (2-3 sentences)
2. **Specific problem** — the specific limitation or gap (2-3 sentences)
3. **Related attempts** — what others have done and their limitations (3-5 sentences, with citations)
4. **Proposed solution** — what this paper does differently (1-2 sentences)
5. **Contributions** — numbered list of specific contributions (3-5 items)
6. **Paper structure** — one sentence on the organisation

## Rules
- Claims about the problem must be supported by references or data
- Contributions must be specific and verifiable from the paper
- Do not overclaim: "first" or "novel" must be explicitly justified
- End with the paper organisation sentence
