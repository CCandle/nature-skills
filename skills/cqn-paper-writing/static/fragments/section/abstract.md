# Abstract

## Structure
1. **Problem** — one sentence on the engineering problem
2. **Existing limitation** — one sentence on why existing approaches fall short
3. **Proposed approach** — one sentence on what you did
4. **Key result** — one sentence with quantitative evidence [CF]
5. **Significance** — one sentence on what the result enables

## Length
150-250 words for a full paper; 100-150 words for a conference paper.

## Rules
- Every substantive claim in the abstract must be supported by evidence
  in the paper body
- Use specific numbers: "10.8 ENOB" not "improved ENOB"
- Define abbreviations at first use
- Do not cite references in the abstract
