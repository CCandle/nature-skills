# Experiment Section Writing

## Structure

A well-written experiment section answers:

1. What was measured? (quantity, range, conditions)
2. How was it measured? (instrument, setup, calibration)
3. Under what conditions? (temperature, load, supply voltage)
4. What were the parameters? (sampling rate, bandwidth, N)
5. How was the data processed? (filtering, windowing, averaging)
