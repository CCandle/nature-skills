# Engineering Paper Style

## IEEE style conventions
- Use IEEE citation format: [1], [2, Fig. 3], [3]-[5]
- Sections: I, II, III (Roman numerals)
- Equations centred, numbered in parentheses
- Figures labelled "Fig. 1", tables labelled "TABLE I"

## General engineering style
- Use active voice for your contributions
- Use passive voice for standard procedures
- Abbreviations: define at first use, use consistently
- Numbers: spell out one through nine, use digits for 10 and above
- Units: use SI, space between number and unit (10 kHz, not 10kHz)

## What to avoid
- "Novel" unless you explicitly contrast with prior work
- "State-of-the-art" unless you cite the current SOTA
- Long paragraphs (>1/3 page)
- First-person plural when referring to yourself alone ("we" is fine)
- Unsupported superlatives
