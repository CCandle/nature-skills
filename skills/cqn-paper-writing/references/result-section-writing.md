# Result Section Writing

## Patterns

### Comparing conditions

"Fig. 5 compares the ADC output spectrum with the LDO Vref (blue) and
the ADR4520 (orange). With the LDO, the noise floor is −85 dBV and the
ENOB is 9.2 bit. With the ADR4520, the noise floor drops to −92 dBV
and the ENOB increases to 10.8 bit."

### Presenting a single key result

"The system achieved a peak throughput of 200 MB/s on the DMA link,
measured over 10,000 transfers of 4 KB each. The throughput was limited
by the AXI bus clock (100 MHz) and the 64-bit bus width."

## Rules

- Present results, not interpretations, first
- Interpretations can follow in the same paragraph
- Every quantitative result must have units
- Include variability: "mean ± std over 10 runs"
