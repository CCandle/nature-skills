# Claim-Evidence Writing

## Pattern

Every paragraph that makes a claim should follow:

1. State the claim
2. Present the evidence (figure reference, table, or data description)
3. Interpret the evidence
4. Tag the claim `[CF]`/`[RH]`/`[UG]`

## Example

"The ADC achieved 10.8 ENOB with the external ADR4520 reference [CF],
compared to 9.2 ENOB with the board LDO [CF] (Fig. 5). This 1.6-bit
improvement confirms that Vref noise was the dominant limitation under
the original configuration."

## Rules

- Do not present a claim without evidence in the same paragraph
- If the evidence is elsewhere in the paper, reference the section
  or figure number
- Use `[CF]` only for claims directly supported by your data
