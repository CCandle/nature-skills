# Limitations Writing

## Why limitations matter

- Reviewers will find them anyway. Acknowledging them proactively
  demonstrates rigour.
- Limitations define the scope of validity for your claims.

## Structure

- One paragraph per limitation
- Each paragraph: what the limitation is → why it exists → what could
  be done about it (future work or mitigation)

## Example

"The ENOB characterisation was performed at room temperature (25 °C).
Temperature drift was not tested. Based on the ADR4520 temperature
coefficient (3 ppm/°C), the Vref drift over 0-70 °C is approximately
0.7 LSB, which would reduce ENOB by approximately 0.3 bit. A full
temperature characterisation is planned as future work."
