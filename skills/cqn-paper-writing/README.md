# cqn-paper-writing

Draft engineering research papers for IEEE, IET, Elsevier engineering
journals, or Chinese core journals.

## Usage

Specify the section or full paper:

> Draft the method section for my ADC characterisation paper. Here are
> the system details and experiment records...

Output: structured draft with claim-evidence alignment.

## Key rules

- Every claim tagged [CF]/[RH]/[UG]
- Missing evidence marked, not swept under the rug
- Engineering style, not Nature style
