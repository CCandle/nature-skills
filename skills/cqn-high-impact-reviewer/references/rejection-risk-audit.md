# Rejection Risk Audit

Pre-submission audit of common rejection reasons from an engineering
perspective.

## Check each dimension

| Dimension | What to check |
|---|---|
| Insufficient contribution | Is the advance clearly distinguished from prior work? |
| Unclear writing | Are methods reproducible from the description? |
| Weak empirical effect | Is the improvement meaningful (not just statistically significant)? |
| Incomplete evaluation | Are baselines fair and ablations complete? |
| Problematic method | Are there technical flaws or unrealistic assumptions? |
| Missing parameters | Are sampling rate, calibration, hardware version declared? |
| Overclaimed results | Are [RH]/[UG] claims presented as [CF]? |
