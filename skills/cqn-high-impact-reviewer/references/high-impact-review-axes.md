# High-Impact Review Axes

## Reviewer 1: Technical validity
- Is the method technically sound?
- Are the experiments correctly designed and controlled?
- Are the conclusions supported by the data?
- Are there technical flaws?

## Reviewer 2: Novelty and significance
- How does the work compare to prior art?
- Is the advance significant for the field?
- Would the results interest a broad readership?
- Is the novelty claim supported by evidence?

## Reviewer 3: Presentation and reproducibility
- Is the paper clearly written?
- Are all experimental details provided for reproducibility?
- Are figures and data presentation clear?
- Is the reproducibility checklist complete?
